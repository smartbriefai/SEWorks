Who We Are

About SEW

Shanthi Engineering Works (SEW) is a Chennai-based firm with over three decades of experience in erecting and assembling RADAR towers, antennas and radomes, along with the supporting electrical works.

Founded by S. Rangasamy, SEW works mainly as a subcontractor to RADAR suppliers and manufacturers, and occasionally directly for the RADAR owner, carrying out the site erection and assembly work as per the drawings and instructions provided. Testing, calibration, commissioning and acceptance of the RADAR remain with the RADAR supplier and the end user.

What We Do

Our Services

Site erection, assembly and supporting electrical work for RADAR installations, carried out mainly as a subcontractor to the RADAR supplier.

RADAR Erection & Assembly

Erection of RADAR towers, masts and lattice steel structures, and assembly of RADAR antennas and associated equipment, as per the RADAR supplier's drawings and instructions.

Radome Installation & Maintenance

Installation of radome panels and maintenance work on existing radomes, as per the RADAR supplier's or owner's specification.

Supporting Electrical Works

Electrical works that support the RADAR installation at site, as per the RADAR supplier's drawings and instructions.

Dismantling & Relocation

Dismantling of RADAR antennas, towers and associated structures when a RADAR is to be relocated or decommissioned, and re-erection at the new site, as directed by the RADAR supplier or owner.

Air Traffic RADAR Sites

Erection and assembly work for Air Traffic Control RADARs, such as ASR and ARSR, at airports and airfields, as per the RADAR supplier's drawings and instructions.

Weather & Defence RADAR Sites

Erection and assembly work for weather RADARs, including Doppler weather RADARs (DWSR), and for defence RADARs, as per the RADAR supplier's drawings and instructions.
